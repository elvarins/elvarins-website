About Elvarins

Elvarins is the online retail brand of Shahabaz Global Trading LLC, a Wyoming-registered business focused on Toys & Entertainment, Pet Supplies, and Health & Household. We bring these departments together around a simple purpose: helping customers find products that fit their interests and everyday needs.

Our approach centers on clear product information, careful assortment selection and direct customer support. For product questions or business inquiries, our team can be reached at sales@elvarins.com.
