Know before you purchase

Clear shopping information.

Shipping & processing

We ship within the United States. Confirmed orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment. Processing time is separate from carrier transit time.

Shipping charges and estimated delivery time are confirmed before purchase based on the delivery address and package. Any applicable taxes are also confirmed before payment.

Returns & refunds

Contact us within 30 days of delivery to request a return. Change-of-mind returns must be unused, unopened and in their original packaging.

Customers pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns. We cover return shipping for damaged, defective or incorrect items. Refunds are issued after return inspection. Contact us for approval and the return address before sending an item back.

Purchasing & product details

Product cards show the price and selling unit. Each listed price covers one retail item in its original packaging. Manufacturer sets are sold as the complete retail package described.

Use “Request purchase details” on a product to reach our team. We confirm availability and the order total before purchase. An inquiry is not an order, and online checkout is not yet enabled. Never email payment card details.

Questions about a product?

Email the item number or use its purchase-details link. Our team will confirm the product, shipping charges and next steps.

Privacy

This site has no customer account or online payment form. Product search and filters operate in your browser. Shipping checks send the selected item, quantity and delivery ZIP code to our server. When carrier quoting is enabled, the ZIP code and package details are sent to our shipping provider to obtain estimates; a complete address is required before purchase. When you email us, we use the details you provide to respond to your inquiry and manage any confirmed order. Please do not send payment card details by email. Google Fonts and the product-image provider receive technical requests when their resources load; our hosting provider may also process technical request information.
